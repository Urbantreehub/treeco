# Google Review Request Templates — Urban Tree Services

Ready-to-send templates in the owner's voice: warm, genuine, Kiwi, low-pressure.

**Before you send anything:** replace every `{review link}` with your real Google review short link. See `how-to-get-your-review-link.md` for how to grab it. Where a template says `{first name}` or `{crew member}`, swap in the real name.

> **Google's rules — read this once.** Never offer a discount, entry into a draw, koha, or anything else in exchange for a review. That's called "review gating"/incentivising and it breaches Google's policies — reviews can get removed and the profile can be penalised. Also don't ask only your happy customers while filtering out unhappy ones. Just ask everyone, honestly, and let people say what they think. These templates are written to stay well inside those rules.

## SMS templates

Keep texts short. All three are under 320 characters. Send from the mobile the customer already knows (027 203 1446) so it looks like a real person, not a marketing blast.

### SMS 1 — Default (send right after the job's done)

> Hi {first name}, thanks for having Urban Tree Services out today — great to help you out. If you were happy with how it went, a quick Google review would mean a lot to our small team. Only takes a minute: {review link}. Cheers, Josh.

### SMS 2 — Warmer "great to meet you" variant

> Hi {first name}, Josh here from Urban Tree Services. Really enjoyed meeting you today and getting that job sorted. If you've got a spare minute, we'd be genuinely grateful for a Google review — it helps other locals find us: {review link}. Thanks heaps!

### SMS 3 — Follow-up nudge (~3 days later, only if no review yet)

> Hi {first name}, just a gentle follow-up from Urban Tree Services — no worries at all if you're flat out. If you did have a moment for a quick Google review, here's the link: {review link}. Really appreciate it. Cheers, Josh.

**Notes on the SMS nudge:** only send one follow-up, and only to people who haven't left a review. If they've already reviewed, or asked not to be contacted, leave it there. One polite nudge is friendly; two is nagging.

## Verbal "ask" script — for the crew, on-site at job's end

Keep it natural and relaxed — this isn't a sales pitch. Say it while you're doing the final walk-around with the customer, once they've seen the finished job and seem happy.

**The ask (short version):**

> "Glad you're happy with it! If you get a minute, we'd really appreciate a quick Google review — we're a small team and it makes a big difference. I'll flick you a text with the link so it's easy — no pressure at all."

**Then follow through:** text them SMS 1 (or SMS 2) with the link before you leave the driveway, while it's fresh. That's the single most effective moment to send it.

**If they hesitate or seem unsure:** don't push. Just say:

> "No worries at all — I'll send the link through anyway in case you feel like it later."

**A few ground rules for the crew:**
- Only ask when the customer genuinely seems happy. Never ask someone who's had an issue — sort the issue first.
- Never offer anything in return (no discount, no freebie). It's against Google's rules and we don't need to.
- Ask everyone who's happy, not just the big jobs. Little jobs count the same.
- One ask on-site + one text is plenty. We don't hound people.
