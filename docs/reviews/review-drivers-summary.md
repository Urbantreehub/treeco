# Why Reviews Matter — Urban Tree Services (One-Pager)

The short version of why Step 4 of the marketing plan is worth the effort.

---

## The goal

Rank higher in Google's **local pack** (the "map with 3 businesses" that shows up when someone searches *arborist Wellington* or *tree removal near me*). That local pack is where most tree-work enquiries start, and where the phone calls come from.

---

## How reviews drive local ranking

Google decides who shows in the local pack on three things: **relevance, distance, and prominence.** Reviews feed prominence — and two review signals matter most:

1. **Review count** — how many reviews you have. More reviews = a stronger prominence signal, and more social proof for the human deciding who to call. This is the single number where you're currently behind competitors.

2. **Review recency (velocity)** — how *fresh* and *steady* the reviews are. A business getting a few new reviews every week reads as active and trusted. A business whose last review was eight months ago looks stale, even with a decent total. **A steady drip beats a one-off burst** — which is exactly why the routine matters more than any single request.

Two supporting factors:
- **Star rating** — you're already elite here at **4.9★**. That's a genuine competitive advantage; protect it by keeping the work quality high and responding well to any rare negative.
- **Responding to reviews** — replying to reviews is itself a positive engagement signal to Google and reassures future customers.

---

## The competitive context — this is the whole point

| Business | Approx. Google reviews |
|----------|------------------------|
| Armes (competitor) | ~144 |
| Elite (competitor) | ~83 |
| **Urban Tree Services (you)** | **79** |

- You're at **79 reviews on a stellar 4.9★** — the *quality* is already there.
- **Elite (~83)** is within striking distance — a couple of weeks of consistent asks passes them.
- **Armes (~144)** is the real target. That ~65-review gap is what's holding back your map ranking against the market leader.
- At a realistic **~6–8 new reviews a month** (see `review-routine.md`), you overtake Armes in roughly **4–6 months** — while your 4.9★ rating stays clearly ahead of the pack on quality.

---

## Why it's winnable

- Your rating (4.9★) is already better than volume-heavy competitors — you're not fixing a quality problem, just a *count and recency* problem.
- You do dozens of jobs a month. If even a fraction of happy customers leave a review, the gap closes fast.
- It costs nothing but a habit: ask at the end of every job, send the link, respond to what comes in.

**Bottom line:** you have the reputation; you just haven't been systematically asking for it. Turn that into a weekly routine and the map ranking follows the review count up.

> **Note on the numbers:** competitor review counts (~144 / ~83) are approximate snapshots and drift over time — treat them as the target to beat, not exact figures. Your own count (79) should be checked live on your Google Business Profile.
