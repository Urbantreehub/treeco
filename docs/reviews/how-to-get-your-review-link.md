# How to Get Your Google Review Link — Urban Tree Services

This is the link customers tap to leave you a Google review. You only need to set it up **once**, then paste it into every template in `review-request-templates.md`.

There are two easy ways to get it. Use whichever is simpler for you.

---

## Method A — From your Google Business Profile (recommended)

1. On your phone or computer, make sure you're signed in to the Google account that manages **Urban Tree Services** on Google.
2. Go to **google.com/business** (or just search "Urban Tree Services" in Google while signed in as the owner — your business dashboard appears right in the search results).
3. Look for the **"Ask for reviews"** or **"Get more reviews"** button. (Google moves this around — it's usually on the main business dashboard, sometimes under a "Reviews" or "Customers" section.)
4. Tap it. Google shows you a **short link** and usually a **"Copy link"** button.
5. Tap **Copy link**. That's your review link — it's now on your clipboard, ready to paste.

The link Google gives you looks like this:

```
https://g.page/r/XXXXXXXXXXXX/review
```

The `XXXX` part is unique to your business. **Don't try to guess or type it** — always copy the real one Google gives you, or it won't work.

---

## Method B — Build it from your Place ID (backup method)

If you can't find the button, the review link always follows this format:

```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID
```

1. Go to Google's **Place ID Finder** (search "Google Place ID Finder" — it's a free Google Maps tool).
2. Type in **Urban Tree Services, Wellington** and select your business on the map.
3. It shows your **Place ID** (a long code like `ChIJ...`).
4. Paste that code in place of `YOUR_PLACE_ID` in the link above.

Both methods point to the same "write a review" screen. Method A's `g.page/r/...` link is shorter and nicer to share, so prefer that.

---

## Shortening the link (optional but tidy)

The `g.page/r/...` link is already fairly short, so you often don't need to do anything. But if you want a clean, memorable link (e.g. for the truck or a business card):

1. Go to a free link shortener — **TinyURL** (tinyurl.com) is simple and doesn't need an account.
2. Paste your Google review link in.
3. Optionally set a custom ending, e.g. `tinyurl.com/urbantree-review`.
4. Click **Make TinyURL** and copy the result.

Now you have a short, human-friendly link you can even read out over the phone.

> **Tip:** Whichever link you settle on, **test it once yourself** — tap it on your phone and check it opens straight to the "rate Urban Tree Services" star screen. Then you know it's right before you send it to customers.

---

## Where to paste it

Open `review-request-templates.md` and replace **every** `{review link}` with your link. It appears in:
- 3 SMS templates
- 2 email templates

If you use a texting app or your job software with a saved template, paste it in there once and you're set.

---

## Make a QR code (great for invoices, cards, and the truck)

A QR code lets someone point their phone camera at it and go straight to your review screen — no typing.

1. Go to a free QR generator (search "free QR code generator" — e.g. qr-code-generator.com or the QR feature built into many link shorteners).
2. Paste in your Google review link (the `g.page/r/...` or TinyURL one).
3. Download the QR code image (PNG).
4. Add it to:
   - **Invoices / quotes** — a small QR with "Happy with the job? Scan to leave us a Google review."
   - **Business cards** — on the back.
   - **The truck / signage** — big enough to scan from a metre or so away.
   - **A little card** the crew can hand over at the end of a job.

> Keep the wording neutral — "Scan to review us on Google" is fine. Never write anything offering a reward for a review (against Google's rules).
