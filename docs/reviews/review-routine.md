# The Review Routine — Urban Tree Services

A simple, repeatable system so review requests actually happen every week instead of being forgotten. The goal: close the gap on competitors (Urban 79 vs Armes ~144) by steadily adding fresh reviews.

---

## The one rule that matters most

**Ask right after the job is done, while the customer is happy and the work is fresh.** This single habit drives 90% of the result. A review request sent three weeks later gets ignored; one sent from the driveway gets done.

---

## When to ask

- **Best moment:** at job completion, during the final walk-around when the customer says they're happy.
- **Follow-through:** send the SMS with the review link **before you leave the property**, or the same evening at the latest.
- **One nudge:** if there's no review after ~3 days, send the SMS 3 follow-up (once only).
- **Don't ask** if the customer had a complaint or seemed unhappy — sort the problem first, then it's fine to ask later once they're satisfied.

---

## Who asks

- **The crew on-site** gives the quick verbal ask at the end of the job (see the script in `review-request-templates.md`).
- **Whoever runs the phone (Josh / office)** sends the text with the link, and handles the 3-day follow-up nudge.
- Decide this once so it's clear and nobody assumes the other person did it. Simple split: **crew asks, office sends + tracks.**

---

## How to track it

You don't need fancy software. A shared note, spreadsheet, or a column in your job system works. Track just enough to not drop anyone:

| Date | Customer | Job | Asked on-site? | Link sent? | Nudge sent (3d)? | Review left? |
|------|----------|-----|----------------|------------|------------------|--------------|
| 15/07 | J. Smith | Gum removal, Karori | Yes | Yes | — | Yes |
| 16/07 | R. Patel | Hedge trim, Newtown | Yes | Yes | Pending | Not yet |

- Tick "Link sent?" the day of the job.
- Each Monday, scan for anyone still "Not yet" after 3+ days and send the nudge.
- Watch your Google review count climb — that's the real scoreboard.

---

## Realistic cadence (this is very achievable)

- Even **3–4 review asks a week ≈ ~15 a month.**
- Not everyone reviews — a good honest ask converts maybe **1 in 3 to 1 in 2**. Say you send 15 asks and get 6–8 reviews a month.
- At **~6–8 new reviews a month**, you go from 79 today to overtaking Armes (~144) within roughly **4–6 months** — and you pass Elite (~83) within the first few weeks.
- The maths only works if it's **consistent**. Three a week, every week, beats a burst of twenty then nothing. Build the habit, protect it.

---

## How to respond to reviews

Responding matters almost as much as getting them. It shows Google (and future customers) that you're active and you care. **Reply to every review — good and bad — ideally within a day or two.**

**For positive reviews:**
- Thank them by name, mention the specific job if you can, keep it warm and human.
- Don't copy-paste the exact same reply to everyone — vary it.

**For critical / negative reviews:**
- Stay calm and polite. Never argue, never get defensive, never blame the customer.
- Acknowledge, apologise if warranted, and offer to make it right offline (give your number).
- A calm, professional reply to a bad review often impresses future readers *more* than the complaint hurts. It shows how you handle problems.

### Example response — 5-star review

> Customer wrote: *"Josh and the team removed a big gum from our back section. Turned up on time, worked safely, and left the place spotless. Would highly recommend."*

**Your reply:**

> Thanks so much, Sarah — really appreciate you taking the time. That gum was a big one! Glad we could get it sorted safely and leave things tidy for you. Give us a shout any time you need a hand. Cheers, Josh & the Urban Tree Services team.

### Example response — critical review

> Customer wrote: *"Job was okay but the crew ran late and didn't let me know. Ended up waiting around most of the morning."*

**Your reply:**

> Hi Mark, thanks for the honest feedback and I'm sorry we kept you waiting — that's on us, and we should have called ahead to let you know. Communication on timing is something we're tightening up. If you're happy to, I'd like to make it right — give me a call on 027 203 1446. Appreciate you giving us the chance to do better. Cheers, Josh.

## Quick weekly checklist

- [ ] Every completed job this week got a verbal ask + a texted link
- [ ] Anyone "not yet" after 3 days got one polite nudge
- [ ] Every new review (good or bad) got a reply within a day or two
- [ ] Glanced at the Google review count — is it climbing?

Keep this on repeat. Steady beats spectacular.
